# Assign receiver dials and Quick shortcuts

SDr Scanner V10 keeps the IC-R8600’s division of scan, listening, memory and tuning controls. **MENU → SET → Dials** lets you change what each dial does when the operating screen is open. **MENU → SET → Quick Menu** lets you choose which shortcuts appear under **QUICK**. Both assignments are saved in the browser. The controls draw on the IC-R8600’s contextual dials, the Yaesu FTDX10’s retained rotary assignments and the Kenwood TS-890S’s programmable shortcuts. [Icom IC-R8600 manual, pp. 1-1–1-6 and 9-1–9-3](https://www.icomeurope.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/IC-R8600_ENG_IM_8.pdf) · [Yaesu FTDX10 manual, pp. 19 and 36–39](https://www.yaesu.com/Files/4CB893D7-1018-01AF-FA97E9E9AD48B50C/FTDX10_OM_ENG_EH076H202_2307A-LS-1.pdf) · [Kenwood TS-890S manual, pp. 16-2–16-3](https://manuals.jvckenwood.com/download/files/B5A-4695-00.pdf)

## Dial A sets scan timing and Dial B controls listening

The IC-R8600 uses Dial A for scan pause and opens separate speed, priority interval and resume settings with a press. Its Dial B defaults to AF volume and offers RF gain, squelch, treble and bass. The Uniden SDS200 uses its scroll encoder to change direction during scan or search and to step through channels or frequencies in hold. [Icom IC-R8600 manual, pp. 1-6 and 9-1–9-3](https://www.icomeurope.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/IC-R8600_ENG_IM_8.pdf) · [Uniden SDS200 manual, pp. 14–15](https://www.uniden.info/download/ompdf/SDS200om.pdf#page=21)

In V10, **Dial A** can adjust pause time, analogue scan speed, resume time, priority interval or the direction of an active scan. **Dial B** can adjust volume, RF gain, squelch, bass, treble, beep level or up to **24 dB** of Audio Boost. Pressing Dial B and selecting a control also assigns it to normal rotation. Audio Boost raises live listening gain in the current browser. Squelch controls when audio opens, and RF gain changes the receiver input level. [SDR++ user guide, pp. 26 and 39](https://www.sdrpp.org/manual.pdf#page=40)

## Dial C separates fine tuning from display span

The IC-R8600 uses Dial C to browse memories and change contextual settings. The Yaesu FTDX10 has a separate rotary with selectable memory, tuning-step and receive fine-adjustment roles. In V10, **Dial C** can browse memories in Memory mode, tune by **10 Hz**, change the tuning step, adjust the scope span or reference level, or change analogue scan speed. **Tuning Step** sets the increment for subsequent normal tuning and **Scope Span** changes the frequency range visible on the display. [Icom IC-R8600 manual, pp. 1-6 and 3-2–4-4](https://www.icomeurope.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/IC-R8600_ENG_IM_8.pdf) · [Yaesu FTDX10 manual, pp. 36–39](https://www.yaesu.com/Files/4CB893D7-1018-01AF-FA97E9E9AD48B50C/FTDX10_OM_ENG_EH076H202_2307A-LS-1.pdf) · [SDR++ user guide, pp. 28–32](https://www.sdrpp.org/manual.pdf#page=31)

The **Main Dial** in V10 can use normal frequency tuning, fixed **10 Hz** or **10 kHz** tuning, tuning-step selection, memory channels, scan direction or scope span. The IC-R8600 offers normal tuning with Auto TS acceleration and a dedicated M-CH DIAL switch. V10 carries those controls forward, and M-CH DIAL can temporarily give the main dial its memory role. Menus continue to use their contextual dial controls. [Icom IC-R8600 manual, pp. 3-2, 8-2 and 11-3](https://www.icomeurope.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/IC-R8600_ENG_IM_8.pdf)

## QUICK opens enabled shortcuts that fit the current mode

Icom’s IC-R30 offers context-sensitive shortcuts for receive mode, tuning step, RF gain, priority watch, band scope and recording. Kenwood lets an operator assign a front-panel key to an existing function, memory or menu screen. V10 combines these patterns. You can enable shortcuts for tuning and filtering, scans, memories, scope settings, volume and squelch, recording, monitoring history and replay. **Temporary Skip** appears when the scan is paused and the skip function is enabled. DMR Tier III and receive filters appear when the mode supports them. The contextual **Default** action continues to apply to the highlighted setting. [Icom IC-R30 manual, pp. 12–13](https://www.icomeurope.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/IC-R30_ENG_BM_2.pdf) · [Icom IC-R8600 manual, pp. 9-10 and 11-1](https://www.icomeurope.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/IC-R8600_ENG_IM_8.pdf) · [Kenwood TS-890S manual, pp. 16-2–16-3](https://manuals.jvckenwood.com/download/files/B5A-4695-00.pdf)
