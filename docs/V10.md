# SDr Scanner V10 user manual

**Version 10.1.3** · SDr Scanner runs a receive-only RTL-SDR radio on a Linux host. Use its browser controls to tune, scan, decode supported signals, view the spectrum, and record audio.

## Start listening

1. Connect the antenna and USB RTL-SDR to the Ubuntu or Debian receiver host. Follow the [install instructions](../README.md#install-on-ubuntu-or-debian).
2. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8766/` on the host, or use the receiver’s LAN name or IP address on another device. If you installed V10 alongside another scanner service, use the port assigned to V10. The V10 receiver opens at `/`.
3. Press **POWER** to start reception from this tab. Hold **POWER** for one second to return to standby and release this tab’s tuner assignment.
4. Tap the large frequency readout. Enter a frequency in MHz on the LCD keypad or with the keyboard, then press **ENT** or **Enter**. You can enter `28.176812` or the LCD format `28.176.812`. For 198 kHz, enter `0.198`. Direct frequency entry switches the receiver to **AUTO**.
5. Press **MODE** at the right end of the lower button row beneath the LCD to select a receive mode. For example, choose **DMR** to listen for DMR on the frequency you entered.
6. Turn the fixed **VOLUME** knob at the lower right to set listening level. Turn the **Squelch** dial, **D** by default, until it just closes on background noise. With its press action set to **Automatic**, press that dial to toggle **Monitor** and temporarily open squelch.

The panel accepts 100 kHz to 1.75 GHz. Coverage depends on the RTL-SDR and antenna. Below 25 MHz, the receiver uses direct sampling. Use an antenna and a dongle that support those frequencies.

### Volume and mute

Scroll or drag the fixed **VOLUME** knob, or focus it and use the arrow keys. A dial assigned to **AF Volume** adjusts the same listening level. The dedicated knob keeps its volume function permanently. The receiver stays on at zero volume.

Press the knob to mute your browser. **MUTE** appears at the upper right of the local LCD and the knob’s indicator turns amber. Press it again to restore audio. By default, mute dims the local LCD to 10% of its normal brightness. Set **MENU → SET → Display → Mute Button Dim → OFF** to mute at your usual brightness. The second LCD keeps its brightness and continues to show receiver activity.

Volume, mute, mute dimming and audio boost are local to your browser. They survive a refresh. An active tab can resume its listening session after refresh. A new tab opens in standby. Tabs listening through the same tuner share tuning and receiver settings.

## Controls, menus and dials

**MENU** opens the main LCD screen. Its buttons are **SCOPE**, **SCAN**, **MEMORY**, **RECORD**, **HISTORY** and **SET**, with **DECODE** shown in DMR or FSK mode. The menu also shows the version. **GitHub** opens the maintainer’s profile and **User Manual** opens this guide in a new tab.

| Front-panel control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **POWER** | Press the round switch to power on. Hold while on to return to standby. The LED ring pulses with live receive audio, including when the browser is muted, and dims when reception goes quiet. A tap while on captures a PNG if **MENU → SET → Function → Screen Capture · POWER Switch** is enabled. |
| **MODE** | Choose the receive mode. The active mode appears at the upper left of the LCD. |
| **REC** | Arm or stop MP3 recording. Amber means armed and red means recording. |
| **SCAN** / **S.SCAN** / **PRIO** | Open or stop a scan, search from the current frequency, or check a priority memory. |
| **LOCK OUT / CLEAR** | Skip the current frequency, or toggle skip on a recalled memory. Hold to clear temporary lockouts. |
| **▲GRP** / **▼GRP** | Step through populated groups in memory mode. Open group selection while in VFO. |
| **V/M** / **MW** | Switch between VFO and memory, or open memory writing. |
| **M-CH DIAL** | Toggle the main dial between memory stepping and its assigned function. |
| **FUNCTION** | Open reception controls for the active mode. |
| **M.SCOPE** | Show or hide the scope. Use **MENU → SCOPE** to open it expanded. |
| **QUICK** | Open the configured shortcuts. |
| **EXIT** | Go back one level. On the main display it leaves the expanded scope. |
| **SPEECH / LOCK** | Tap to hear the frequency and mode spoken. Hold to lock or unlock the main tuning dial. **MENU → SET → Function → SPEECH/LOCK Switch** can swap those gestures. |
| **RECEIVE** / **LOCK** | Indicate open receive audio or monitor, and main-dial lock respectively. |

### Navigate the LCD

Tap a menu button or turn **Dial C** to highlight an item and press it to select. The other configurable dials and the main dial can also select a highlighted item. Use on-screen **▲**, **▼** and **↩** where shown, or use keyboard arrows, **Enter** and **Esc**. **EXIT** and right-click on a button or dial also go back. Right-clicking a populated favourite instead opens its clear prompt.

In an editor, highlight a field and push **Dial C** to open its choices or keypad. **ENT** accepts keypad input. Use **SET**, **START**, or **MW** to apply the form. You can also type directly into an editable field. If a dial opens its assigned setting, turn it to adjust the value or move through the choices. Hover over a control for its description.

### Assign the dials

Turn a dial by scrolling over it or dragging it. Arrow keys also turn a focused dial. Hold **A–J** or the **main dial** for one second to open its settings, or use **MENU → SET → Dials**. Choose a function from the list for turning. Open **Press Action** to choose what a short press does while listening. **Automatic** follows the turn function. **None** disables that gesture’s listening shortcut. Inside an LCD menu, a short press selects the highlighted item. Turn, press and sensitivity choices are saved in your browser. The function list shows which other dials already use each assignment.

| Turn function | Automatic short press | Other available press actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frequency, fine or coarse tuning | Open frequency entry | Choose tuning step, toggle tuning lock or switch VFO and memory |
| Tuning Step | Choose the tuning step | Open frequency entry or toggle tuning lock |
| Memory Channel, Preset Channel or FAV Bank A/B | Open the corresponding channel list or preset settings | Memory groups, VFO/memory or preset scan hold, depending on the assignment |
| AF Volume or Squelch | Toggle mute or Monitor, respectively | Open the assigned setting or Audio / RF Settings |
| RF gain, boost, treble, bass or beep level | Open the assigned setting | Audio / RF Settings and reset where offered |
| Scope Pan, Span or Reference | Recenter on the tuned frequency, open Span, or edit the reference level | Scope hold, fixed edges, full capture or marker, depending on the assignment |
| Scan Direction | Reverse a running scan or open the scan menu | Start or stop a scan, or hold a preset scan |
| Scan Speed, Pause/Resume Time or Priority Interval | Open the assigned setting | Scan settings, scan shortcuts or Priority Watch, depending on the assignment |

The automatic choices follow the contextual push-dial controls in the [Icom IC-R8600 manual](https://www.icomeurope.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/IC-R8600_ENG_IM_6.pdf), the [Yaesu FTDX10 manual](https://www.yaesu.com/Files/4CB893D7-1018-01AF-FA97E9E9AD48B50C/FTDX10_OM_ENG_EH076H202_2307A-LS-1.pdf) and [Uniden’s SDS200 waterfall guide](https://www.uniden.info/download/ompdf/SDS200Waterfallom.pdf#page=3).

Assignments include fixed 10 Hz or 10 kHz tuning, tuning step, memory channel, preset channel, favourite bank selection, audio and RF controls, scope pan, span and reference, scan direction and speed, pause and resume time, and priority interval. **Normal** sensitivity uses the usual rate, **High** doubles it, and **Very High** quadruples it.

Assign **Preset Channel** to step through the current preset zone. During a scan, turning the dial holds the channel you step to and respects scan lockouts. Press the scope’s **HOLD** key to resume the scan. When browsing recalled favourites, the dial visits saved channels marked to be skipped during scanning. Memories in Banks 0 and 1 can be browsed together. After recalling a memory from Banks 2–99, the dial browses that bank.

Select **TS** by the meter to choose the main tuning step. Hover over a frequency digit and scroll to change its place value. For example, scrolling up over the **2** in **446.006250 MHz** adds 100 Hz. Double-click the frequency to copy it with the unit. Tap the clock to view the host’s local time and UTC.

## Save and recall channels

- **FAV A** and **FAV B** each have ten direct keys and initially show Banks 0 and 1. Assign **FAV BANK A (Left)** or **FAV BANK B (Right)** to a dial to choose which memory group from 0 to 99 that panel shows. Each selection is saved in the browser. Tap **CH0–CH9** to recall a channel from the displayed group. Hold a key for one second to store the current tune there. Amber marks a saved channel and green marks the current channel. To clear a saved key, right-click it and right-click the same key again to confirm.
- Open **MENU → SET → Favourite Banks** and choose a range to name any bank from **00** to **99**. For quicker access to the two banks currently on the panel, select the **FAV A** or **FAV B** shortcut at the top. Press **SET** to save, or save an empty name to restore the numbered heading. The name follows the numbered bank and is included in settings backups.
- Tap **MW → Select the Channel and Write** to enter the group, slot, frequency, mode and optional name. Enter the relevant tone or digital filters, then press **MW** in the LCD editor. If the slot is occupied, confirm replacement. Hold the front-panel **MW** to save the current tune in the first free slot of the selected group when available.
- **MENU → MEMORY** opens the group browser. Use **GROUP** to choose a group or **CH-INP** to recall a numbered channel. Open a memory to edit its name and scan tag, set or clear its skip flag, or delete it.
- **V/M** toggles VFO and memory. In memory mode, hold it to move the current tune into VFO. Turn a dial assigned to **Memory Channel**, or enable **M-CH DIAL** and then turn the main dial to step through memories.
- **FAV** in the preset grid scans Banks 0 and 1. To scan other groups, choose **MEMO** under **SCAN**. Group A is reserved for Auto Memory Write scan hits.

## Scan zones and search

### Preset zones

Tap a **PRESETS** key to start scanning its ranges or channels. Tap other keys to add them. The lit selections and the LCD’s **PRESETS** row show the active zones. Tap an active key to remove it. Tap the last one or press **SCAN** to stop.

| Preset | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| **FAV** | Saved memories in Banks 0 and 1 |
| **CB**, **PMR** | The UK CB channel allocations and PMR446 frequencies, with separate entries for supported modes |
| **BUSINESS**, **AMATEUR** | Separate UK band ranges with band-specific tuning steps and receive modes |
| **FM RADIO** | Broadcast FM from 87.5 to 107.9 MHz on a 100 kHz grid |
| **RAIL**, **EVENTS**, **UTILITIES** | Scan windows based on registered assignments |
| **AIR**, **MARINE** | Civil AM airband and marine VHF and onboard UHF voice ranges |
| **CUSTOM 1–12** | Your named frequency ranges in AUTO mode |
| **NEARBY** | Strong-signal watch across supported voice bands while you continue listening |

If **FM RADIO** is the only selected preset and you are already inside that band, the scan starts at the next grid frequency in the current direction and wraps at the band edge. Turn a dial assigned to **Scan Direction** to continue past a station.

Hold a preset key or open **MENU → SET → Presets** to edit its scan ranges. Choose a range or **Add Range** and set its start, end, step and receive mode. **Restore Default Ranges** returns that preset to its factory limits. Open **Stored Channels** to recall, edit or delete individual entries. **CB** and **PMR** open their editable channel lists directly. A scan across overlapping zones can check the same frequency and mode more than once.

The amateur default uses separate ranges for each supported UK band, including eleven 60 m windows and 23 cm coverage. The band limits come from the RSGB reference data and the scanner uses band-dependent grid spacing. [RSGB Full reference data](https://rsgb.services/public/exams/exam-forms/260218_EX309_Full_2026_v1.pdf) · [RSGB operating band plans](https://rsgb.org/main/operating/band-plans/)

Rail, events and utilities use receive windows around registered assignments. Events also includes the Channel 21 portable and base ranges. [Ofcom Wireless Telegraphy Register](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/spectrum/frequencies/wtr) · [Ofcom Channel 21](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/spectrum/radio-equipment/channel-21)

Configure **Custom 1–12** from their keys or **MENU → SET → Presets**. Enter the name and lower and upper frequencies. **Scan Step → Auto** uses 8.33 kHz for a range wholly inside civil airband, 100 kHz for a range wholly inside broadcast FM, 1 kHz below 30 MHz, and 6.25 kHz for other ranges. Choose an explicit step if the channels use a different grid. Custom ranges can run alongside other zones.

### Nearby, hold and scan speed

Tap **NEARBY** to start or stop watching for strong signals while tuned or during a preset scan. It checks the live capture around the tuned channel and sweeps other voice bands, revisiting PMR446 frequently. It picks the strongest eligible channel in each checked window. With one tuner, checks outside the live capture can briefly interrupt the audio. The sweep line moves across the scope as it searches. With only Nearby active, **LISTENING** can appear on the readout and waterfall. On a hit, the panel shows **NEARBY RX** and uses AUTO. It returns to your previous tuning seven seconds after the reception ends. A reply restarts the delay. Press the scope’s **HOLD** to stay on a hit, then press it again to resume. Hold the **NEARBY** key to see and tune previous hits.

A single tuner can briefly interrupt audio to inspect signals outside its current RF window. An available second tuner can run Nearby checks while the first keeps receiving.

While scanning presets, press **HOLD** on scope page **<1>** to pause or resume. Selecting a point on the spectrum or waterfall also pauses the preset scan to inspect that frequency. Moving away from a digital preset can switch inspection to AUTO. Resuming returns to the preset’s stored mode. **HOLD** or **SCAN HOLD** indicates an engaged hold. On page **<2>**, **SPEED** sets the preset scan rate. A preset scan stays on an open signal, then waits for the configured **Resume Timer** after it closes.

Turn on **MENU → SCAN → SET → Scan Follows Scope** to link scan rate to the selected spectrum span. It applies to S.SCAN, programmed ranges, presets and memory scans. Spans up to 100 kHz use the base **Scan Speed**. Wider spans increase the target rate up to twice the base speed at 1.8 MHz, capped at 30 checks per second. Mode acquisition times can reduce the actual rate. Tuning steps, open-signal holds and pause or resume timers keep their existing behaviour. The receiver setting defaults to **OFF**. With it enabled, changing the selected span while scanning updates that scan's rate.

### Scan types, priority and lockouts

| Control | Operation |
| --- | --- |
| **SCAN → PROG** | Search within stored scan edges or a configured frequency range. |
| **SCAN → ΔF** | Search within a span around a centre frequency. |
| **SCAN → AUTO** | Search a range and save accepted hits in memory Group A. |
| **SCAN → MEMO** | Scan stored memories, optionally limited to a group. |
| **SCAN → SEL** | Scan memories marked with a select tag. |
| **SCAN → MODE** | Scan memories with a specified mode. |
| **S.SCAN** | Start searching up from the current frequency with the current tuning step. Hold to start down. While running, turn the main dial or hold **S.SCAN** to change direction. Tap to stop. |
| **PRIO** | Check the selected memory at the configured interval. Hold during priority watch to stop the watch and recall that memory. |
| **LOCK OUT / CLEAR** | Exclude a frequency while scanning or tuning manually. With scanning stopped on a memory, toggle its saved skip flag. Hold to clear temporary lockouts. |

Memory, select-tag and mode scans need at least two eligible memories. **SCAN** stops the running scan. Hold it while idle to reuse the last scan. Open **MENU → SCAN → SET** for pause, resume, speed, temporary skip and main-dial behaviour. **FINE** uses 10 Hz steps during a programmed or delta-frequency scan. **RECALL** returns to the previous frequency. Timed **SKIP** expires after the configured interval. Temporary frequency lockouts survive a scan restart and clear when the receiver returns to standby.

## Receive modes and decoding

| Mode | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **AUTO** | Select WFM in the broadcast FM band and AM in civil airband or the configured long- and medium-wave broadcast bands. Elsewhere starts with narrow FM and can classify strong signals as AM or FM. |
| **FM** / **WFM** | Narrow FM voice and wide FM broadcasting respectively. |
| **AM** / **S-AM(D/U/L)** | AM voice or broadcast reception, or synchronous AM using both, upper or lower sidebands. |
| **USB** / **LSB** | Single-sideband signals. |
| **CW** / **CW-R** | Morse code with normal or reverse beat-note direction. |
| **FSK** / **FSK-R** | RTTY using normal or reversed mark and space. **MENU → DECODE** shows the ITA2 text and decode settings. |
| **DMR**, **D-STAR**, **P25**, **dPMR**, **NXDN-VN** | Supported conventional digital voice modes. Select the protocol explicitly. P25 is Phase I and NXDN-VN is very narrowband. Reception depends on decoder, hardware and signal quality. |

Tap **FIL** to choose a filter or set bandwidth where supported. **FUNCTION** shows mode-specific controls such as AGC, notch, noise blanker, noise reduction, tone or digital squelch. **MENU → SET → Tone Control** adjusts the audio filters and EQ exposed for the selected mode. **Audio Boost** adds up to 24 dB to live audio in this browser.

For FM tones, open **FUNCTION → TONE → T-SCAN** to detect CTCSS or DCS. Recall an FM memory and use **T-SQL / DTCS** to enter its matching tone or code. A CTCSS entry looks like `123.0` and a DCS entry like `D023N`. For P25 or NXDN, **FUNCTION → D.SQL** can filter by NAC or RAN.

To view broadcast FM station data, select **WFM** and turn on **MENU → SET → Display → RDS**. The station name and RadioText appear when the signal carries decodable RDS.

### DMR voice, SMS and Tier III

1. Select **MODE → DMR** and tune to a receive frequency. Open **MENU → DECODE → Conventional → Program Channel** to save it. You can leave **Colour Code**, **Time Slot** and **Talkgroup** blank or fill them in to filter the memory. **D.SQL** separately lets you select any, group or individual calls.
2. Read supported received SMS under **MENU → DECODE → DMR SMS** or **MENU → HISTORY → DMR SMS**. Open a message for its sender, destination, frequency and text. Use **Delete** and confirm to remove the message.
3. For a Tier III site, open **MENU → DECODE → Tier III → Site Set**. Enter the control channel in MHz and a comma-separated LCN-to-frequency map. Include the control frequency in the map. For example, use the format `101=460.125, 102=460.225`, substituting the actual site values. Add any talkgroup names, hold, block or allow-list rules, then select **SET**.
4. Select **START** to follow the configured site. A single tuner moves from the control channel to a mapped traffic channel for supported clear group voice and back to control afterwards. The screen shows the control and traffic channels when known. Stop with **STOP** or **SCAN**.

The app is receive-only and plays clear digital calls. It uses DSD-neo on the receiver host. Run the complete [installer](../README.md#run-the-full-installer) to set up the bundled decoder.

## Spectrum, waterfall and second display

- **<1>** and **<2>** cycle between the scope control pages. **SPAN** and dials assigned to **Scope Span** let you zoom from 12.5 kHz out to the full live capture, up to 1.8 MHz. **Full capture** shows the entire SDR window, whose midpoint can differ from the tuned frequency. **CENT/FIX** fixes the displayed edges or returns to a view centred on the tuned frequency. The centred view fits the span to the available capture to fill the live scope. **FIX FIT** indicates that the saved edges extend outside the live capture and the displayed range has been adjusted. The saved edges return when the SDR covers them again. Use **EDGE** to edit those limits or take them from the current capture.
- Turn **F · Scope Pan**, to the left of the main dial, to move the spectrum and waterfall within the SDR’s live capture while the radio stays on the tuned frequency. Each turn moves by 10% of the visible width at normal sensitivity. Use **SPAN** to zoom in before panning when the whole capture is visible. Press **F** to return to the centred view. The panned view is remembered in the browser.
- **HOLD** freezes or resumes the scope during ordinary tuning. Hold it to clear the stored peak trace. During a preset scan or a Nearby hit, the same button controls the receiver hold instead.
- **REF** adjusts the reference level. **SPEED** cycles scope update speed unless a preset scan is active. **MARKER** toggles the peak marker. Tap **EXPD/SET** to switch between the regular display and a view with the spectrum, waterfall, and scope controls. Hold it to open scope settings, including Max Hold, averaging, waterfall visibility and waterfall speed.
- Hover over the plot for the frequency at the pointer. Tap or click to tune to it. A wheel notch over the plot adjusts tuning by 1% of the displayed span. Previously captured waterfall rows keep their original frequency positions when you retune.
- The dashed **SQL** line shows the receiver’s squelch threshold in dBFS. The white diamond on the tuned frequency shows the channel power compared with that threshold. The blue trace shows individual FFT bins, so a signal spread across several bins can open squelch even when its highest bin is below the line. The bar shows signal strength above the receiver noise floor. **QUICK → Meter Type** switches the number beside it between dBFS and relative strength. The SQL line disappears when receiver squelch is fully open.

Open `/lcd` on a phone, computer or HDMI monitor for a read-only view of the active receiver. It opens without a PIN and shows the current frequency, selected zones including custom names, and the spectrum and waterfall, with no audio or tuner controls. **QUICK → Second Display** opens the same address and, in the same browser, can link it to the selected control tab and show its menus. If the control tab uses local audio mute, the second LCD continues to show receiver activity at normal brightness.

## Recordings, history and replay

1. To choose where clips go, open **MENU → RECORD → Recorder Set → REC FOLDER**. Enter a writable directory on the Linux receiver host and press **SET**.
2. Press **REC** beside **LOCK OUT** to arm it. The receiver starts a clip when eligible audio opens squelch. Press **REC** again to disarm. Completed clips of at least two seconds are saved as MP3 files directly in the chosen directory.
3. Open **MENU → RECORD → Play Files** and select the latest file. **STOP** returns to live audio. When history audio is available, **Instant Replay** plays the most recent captured activity. It can also be added to **QUICK**.

V10 uses the receiver host’s local time and a filename such as `14:09:15 - 15112026 - 443.00625.mp3`.

Open **MENU → HISTORY → RX History** for recent activity. Choose an entry to see capture duration, station or memory name, received or detected mode, tone or code, peak signal, SNR and estimated carrier offset when available. Digital entries can also show radio IDs, talkgroups and other decoded information. **PLAY** plays captured audio. **REC** saves an eligible captured clip in the recording directory. Logging starts when V10 powers on. **Pause Log** and **Resume Log** control new entries. History is held in memory. Save clips you want to keep before restarting the scanner service.

## Connect from another device

A trusted, direct LAN connection or known Tailscale peer can open the scanner without entering a PIN. See the [private network access instructions](../README.md#access-from-another-computer) for supported hostnames, routed LANs and port configuration. Each active tuner can receive one frequency at a time. Additional available dongles let the host assign independent tuners to other listening tabs.

### Set a remote PIN

1. On the scanner host, open the panel on its loopback address. Choose **MENU → SET → PIN**. You can set it while listening, scanning or in standby.
2. Enter a six-digit PIN and select **ENT** or **Enter**. Enter it again to save.
3. If you are setting the PIN from another trusted LAN device, run the following on the scanner host. Use the port assigned to the service in both places.

   ```bash
   curl -fsS -X POST -H 'Origin: http://127.0.0.1:8766' http://127.0.0.1:8766/api/access/setup
   ```

   Enter the returned `setup_code` at the PIN prompt, then choose the six digits. The code expires after five minutes and can be used once.

On a connection that requires remote authentication, press **POWER** and enter the PIN to unlock reception. The read-only `/lcd` opens separately. [Remote domain and forwarded-IP access](../README.md#use-a-domain-or-forwarded-ip-address)

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **SDR UNAVAILABLE** | Confirm the dongle is attached to the Linux host. Close other software using that USB device. Return listening tabs to standby before testing with `rtl_test`. |
| The spectrum moves but audio is silent | Press a control to enable browser audio. Check **MUTE**, local **VOLUME**, receiver mode and squelch. Lower the SQL threshold or use **Monitor** to check a weak signal. |
| A scan stops on noise or misses signals | Check the antenna, mode and squelch. Check the preset's start, end and channel grid. Allow time for a full pass across wide ranges. |
| A preset scan stays on one frequency | Check the LCD for **HOLD** or **PAUSE**. Press scope **HOLD** to resume a manual hold. For a signal holding the scan open, turn the assigned **Scan Direction** dial to continue. |
| WFM audio works but RDS is blank | Enable **MENU → SET → Display → RDS**, then give the station time to decode. Check reception and whether the station transmits RDS. |
| Digital decoder reports an error | Run the complete installer on the receiver host, verify your mode and channel settings, and check the signal and service journal. |
| No MP3 appears | Check the receiver host’s configured folder and permissions. Arm **REC** and let an eligible signal finish after at least two seconds. |
| The second LCD is in standby | Power on a control panel with an available tuner. To mirror that tab’s menus as well, use **QUICK → Second Display** in the same browser. |

See the [Icom parity audit](../reports/SDr%20Scanner%20V10%20parity.md) for the software coverage and remaining hardware checks, and the [dial and Quick Menu reference](../reports/Receiver%20dial%20assignments.md) for design references.
